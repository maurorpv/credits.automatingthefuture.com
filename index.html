Credits you can use without a company.

This is not a marketplace and not a $7 million vault. It is a short, dated list of official programs that a person with an email and a side project can actually open.

Read this first

  • We do not give you credits. Providers do. Every apply button goes to their site.
  • There is nothing for sale here. No subscription, no “lifetime access,” no email wall.
  • We do not add up the catalog into a fantasy total. A realistic first month is usually free tiers plus a few signup credits — hundreds of dollars of usage, not six figures.
  • Listings go stale. Each card is marked checked 2026-09-04. Confirm the official page before you plan around a number.
  • Approval is never guaranteed. Several “startup credit” headlines require a legal entity, an accelerator, or a VC intro. Those are parked under skip.

A realistic stack if you are one person

Use this week

Gemini free tier + Groq or Cerebras + a host (Vercel Hobby) + Supabase Free. Optional: Deepgram or fal.ai signup credit when you have a real job for it.

Do not count on

OpenAI’s old $5 trial, “$100k AWS Activate,” “$100k Claude for Startups,” or any page that wants articles of incorporation. Incorporate later if the product needs it — not to unlock a landing-page number.
